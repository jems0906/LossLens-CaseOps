# Neutral Interview Guide

Use role labels and synthetic records in this practice workspace. Identify the purpose of the conversation, explain that the review concerns records and process, and invite corrections. Ask one open question at a time; record answers as reported statements, not established facts.

## Open prompts

- Please walk me through your understanding of the receiving handoff.
- What records or tools did you use, and what did each show at the time?
- Were there delays, corrections, damage reports, or other context we should include?
- What would help us verify the sequence of events?
- Is there anything in our summary that you would correct or clarify?
- Who else or what process records could help explain the handoff, without presuming anyone caused the discrepancy?

## Notes form

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Interviewee role | Synthetic role only; do not enter a real name or badge identifier |
| Date and time | ISO 8601 timestamp with timezone |
| Topics covered | Short neutral topic labels |
| Notes | Accurate summary; distinguish direct quotation from paraphrase |
| Follow-up records requested | Synthetic reference and accountable owner |

Do not use coercion, deception, guilt scoring, or automated credibility analysis. Do not frame missing scans, job assignments, shift differences, or correlation as proof of misconduct.
